## **Welcome to the UCL Data Science Society**

**UCL's largest student-led tech community, 450+ members strong since 2019. Whether you're brand new to coding or already deep into projects, DSS gives you the platform, tools, and connections to grow.**

**We're built around three things: technical upskilling, industry collaboration, and a community that pushes each other forward.**

**Now, come find us at Fresher's Fair!**

## What we do

We're here to make you confident, capable, and connected in the world of tech.

**➤ Upskill Like a Pro**

* Weekly technical workshops covering Python (NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib), machine learning and model evaluation, SQL, and data visualisation
* Take part in **Project Labs** — our flagship 6-week, project-based programme where small teams work end to end on real datasets, from problem framing to a final presentation
* Get mentorship and guidance to go from zero to hireable

**✶ Real Industry Exposure:**

* Past collaborations with **Google, OpenAI, Bloomberg, Microsoft, The Economist, BlackRock, Visa, LEGO, Rockstar Games, Notion, and Macquarie**
* Events like **HackLondon, HackEurope, Bloomberg's Bpuzzled Challenge, the Google x Neuphonic AI Hackathon, and our own Zero to Demo Hackathon with Lovable, Google Cloud, and Eleven Labs**
* 3 hackathons and datathons run with industry partners every term, plus 4-5 career events including panels, fireside chats, and keynote speakers
* Hear directly from engineers, researchers, and alumni who *have done it*

**𓄲 A Community That Has Your Back:**

* Themed socials **(with freebies!)**, skill-sharing sessions, and group study jams
* A welcoming space where your questions are valued and your growth matters — members come from Engineering, Maths & Physical Sciences, Social & Historical Sciences, Arts & Humanities, and beyond, so whatever you study, there's a place for you
* Optional **mentorship** if you're starting from scratch

**⚡︎ A Track Record That Speaks for Itself:**

* Winner of the UCL Students' Union award for **highest contribution to student professional development**, earned by Project Labs in its very first year
* Fortnightly newsletter with curated job tips, research drops, and opportunities
* 4k+ Instagram followers and 2k+ LinkedIn followers, and counting
