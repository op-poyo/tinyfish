We are a community of students passionate about robotics, AI, and technology. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced builder, our society provides the space to learn, create, and connect through hands-on projects, workshops, competitions, and socials.

***🤖 Workshops:***

Across the year, we'll host workshops tailored to different sections:

* **Term 1**: CAD & 3D Printing, Arduino & Sensors, PCB Design, etc.
* **Term 2**: ROS2 Tutorials

***🤖 Competitions & Projects:***

* **Robotics Hackathon:** An exciting event where ideas, teamwork, and innovation come together.
* **Global Hardware Hackathons:** Join teams representing UCL at international event and collaborate with engineers worldwide.
* **Litter Picking Robot:** TBA
* **Unibots:** UK wide competition

***🤖 Industry & Talks:***

We will be inviting guest speakers from academia and industry to share insights on robotics research, real-world applications, and career paths. More details to be announced during the year.

***🤖 Socials:***

* Movie Nights featuring robotics and sci-fi classics
* Collaborations with other UCL societies and other universities
* Informal socials such as pizza nights and pub quizzes to bring members together
